Supported features: box lyre with two arms and yoke; bull-head ornament, gold and lapis on surviving lyres; fringed clothing; patterned long hair and beards in Early Dynastic art. Museum instrument frames include modern reconstruction.
Game adaptations: compact lyre proportions, simplified strings and decoration, cartoon proportions, short fringed wrap, friendly expression and exact carrying grip. The walking procession is not claimed to be documented by the Standard of Ur. Penn's study describes small hand-held lyres as well as larger instruments.
