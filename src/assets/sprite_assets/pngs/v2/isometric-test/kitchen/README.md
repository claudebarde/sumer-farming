# Kitchen artwork trial

Horizontal: one row by two columns. Vertical variant will follow design approval.
Placement must require a connecting road tile with access to the working side.
This is proposed artwork only, not a playable building. Generated using the
built-in image generator from the farmhouse kitchen preview. The surrounding
halo still needs removal before integration.

## Prompt

Create one standalone outdoor kitchen game sprite based on ONLY the little hearth, pot and wooden table at the bottom right of the reference. Do NOT include the farmhouse, walls, roof, awning or ladder. Match exactly the warm golden mudbrick, terracotta, chunky illustrated outlines and detailed soft shading of the reference. Isometric elevated three-quarter view, orthographic, approximately 2:1 ground projection. Horizontal kitchen: footprint ONE row by TWO columns, two adjacent isometric tiles, long axis running from screen upper-left to lower-right. On the left tile a compact low rectangular mudbrick stove with an arched fuel opening facing the viewer's lower-left and stacked firewood within; a large lidded terracotta cooking pot sits on stone supports on top. Immediately alongside on the right tile a low sturdy wooden preparation table with a clay mixing bowl, wooden spoon and small wooden utensils. The two modules have comparable widths and stand on the same ground plane. Leave the long front-facing lower-left side unobstructed for a farmer approaching from an adjacent connecting road tile. No props outside the two-tile footprint. Do not draw the road or any tile or base slab. No person, text, symbols, steam, fire effects, background, scenery or cast shadow. Only one kitchen, centered with transparent margins, full sprite uncropped. Genuine transparent background. High-resolution crisp game artwork.

Follow-up edit: Remove all the brown glowing halo, background haze and ground shadow surrounding this kitchen. Preserve the kitchen artwork exactly. Clean isolated cutout with fully transparent pixels everywhere outside the stove, pot, wooden table and utensils, including gaps between the table legs. No glow, no ground, no background shading. Keep the same composition and orientation.
