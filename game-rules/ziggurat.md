# Neighbourhood ziggurat

> Interest registration, all four construction stages, donations and shared visual
> deliveries, contribution leaderboard and cosmetic completion rewards are playable.
> Festivals remain agreed plans and are not yet playable.

## An optional neighbourhood project

Build a ziggurat together with your neighbours to earn bonuses and unlock
festivals. Building it, contributing to it, or qualifying for its benefits is
never required to level up your farm or continue your individual progression.

## Location and appearance

The ziggurat occupies a fixed 4 × 4 tile site in the neighbourhood square between
the market and the farm shown in the placement reference. Its bottom-left corner
aligns with the bottom-left corner of that road-bounded square.

The finished ziggurat should look larger than both the market and the farm.
Construction happens in multiple visible stages. The completed artwork and its
4 × 4 placement were approved on October 27, 2026. The current neighbourhood
display follows every saved construction stage and ends with the completed ziggurat.

## Opening construction

At least **three level-10 neighbours must express interest** in the ziggurat
popup before fundraising opens. Having three eligible neighbours alone is not
enough. The popup shows interested neighbours out of all current level-10
neighbours, adapting as eligible membership changes.

Fundraising opens immediately when the third neighbour registers interest;
there is no announcement waiting period. Expressing interest is free and does
not grant contribution points or festival access. Actual donations determine
those benefits. The participant list is not frozen.

Interest is saved and shared with the neighbourhood. You can withdraw it before
fundraising opens. Duplicate requests count only once. Once fundraising opens,
it stays open; withdrawal is no longer available. Neighbours below level 10 can
view progress. The opening view displays the first stage's material requirements
and your stored quantities. Eligible neighbours can donate immediately.

## Who can participate?

Any neighbour at level 10 or above can contribute while construction remains
unfinished, including neighbours who reach level 10 during the project.
Lower-level players can see construction progress but cannot contribute or
receive festival benefits.

## First stage: foundations (playable)

Donate **30 clay and 15 reeds** together as a neighbourhood. Each accepted clay
or reed earns **2 contribution points**. You may donate either resource or both
in one confirmation, using stored goods only. Only the remaining requirement is
accepted; excess materials stay in your estate. If you lack the accepted quantity,
the donation is rejected without deducting either material. Repeating a request
after a connection problem never charges the same donation twice.

The final required donation starts construction automatically. Foundations take
**6 hours**, including time away from the game. The site changes to a worksite
with a progress bar and a remaining-time counter in its popup. When the timer
ends, completed foundations appear. Your farmer remains available throughout.
Fundraising for the lower terrace opens immediately when the foundations timer ends.

## Building each stage

There are no reserved shares or personal donation limits. Eligible neighbours
can contribute as much as they wish of the materials still needed for the
current stage.

Construction of each stage starts automatically as soon as all its materials
are available; no separate start approval or reservation waiting period is needed.
Contributions across all stages count toward each player's construction total.

## Donating and visual deliveries

Click the construction site or any stage of the ziggurat to open its donation
popup. Select the materials and quantities to contribute from stored estate
goods, then confirm. Accepted donations are deducted and credited immediately;
the farmer remains free to perform farm work.

Barley donations must leave **at least one barley in estate storage** for
planting, counting the farm, completed granary and warehouse together. Carried
barley, ground piles and growing crops do not count toward this reserve. The
donation limit respects this reserve, and the server checks it again when
accepting the donation.

The neighbourhood provides one shared pack donkey for deliveries, independent
of players' mill donkeys. It rests at a small hitching post beside the neighbourhood
market, clear of roads and the ziggurat footprint.

Each visual delivery follows this sequence:

1. The neighbourhood farmer walks from their farm to fetch the donkey.
2. The farmer leads the donkey back to their farm.
3. A short loading animation adds bags to both sides of the donkey.
4. The farmer leads the loaded donkey along the roads to the ziggurat and unloads.
5. They return the donkey with empty panniers to the hitching post, then the
   farmer walks home.

Mixed materials are combined into one visual load. With only one shared donkey,
delivery trips run one after another across the neighbourhood. Repeated donations
from the same player are combined into their pending or active visual trip rather
than creating additional trips while that trip remains unfinished. Only the
visual delivery waits its turn; every accepted donation is credited immediately.

These delivery movements are visible to other players viewing the same
neighbourhood. They illustrate accepted donations; they do not control when
materials are credited or construction starts. Leaving the view, closing the
tab or missing an animation never delays or reverses a donation.

## Builders and saved progress

Independent neighbourhood builders construct the ziggurat. Your farmer remains
available for normal farm work throughout construction.

Construction continues while players are offline. Confirmed contributions never
expire and cannot be withdrawn.

Only materials still needed for the stage are accepted and deducted. If neighbours
contribute at the same time, the remaining requirement is checked before each
contribution is accepted. You are not charged for excess materials.

## Construction stages

The ziggurat is built in four stages:

| Stage | Visible change | Construction duration after funding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations | Broad brick platform | 6 hours |
| Lower terrace | First raised level | 12 hours |
| Upper terraces | Stepped structure | 18 hours |
| Summit sanctuary | Completed ziggurat | 24 hours |

The four stages take **60 hours of construction** in total, plus the time needed
to gather contributions. Each stage's construction timer starts when its
material requirements are fully funded.

Material costs are fixed for each stage, regardless of the number of neighbours.
Larger groups share the same total requirements among more participants; joining
or leaving does not change the project's material costs.

| Stage | Required materials |
| --- | --- |
| Foundations | 30 clay, 15 reeds |
| Lower terrace | 45 clay, 30 barley, 15 bread |
| Upper terraces | 60 clay, 24 bread, 18 beer |
| Summit sanctuary | 30 clay, 12 baskets of dates, 250 shekels |

Total goods required: **165 clay, 15 reeds, 30 barley, 39 bread, 18 beer and
12 baskets of dates, plus 250 shekels**.

## Contribution points

Each accepted contribution earns fixed points. These values do not change with
market prices and determine ranking in the construction leaderboard. There is
no minimum point or percentage threshold for festival access.

| Resource | Points per unit |
| --- | --- |
| Clay | 2 |
| Reeds | 2 |
| Barley | 3 |
| Bread | 6 |
| Beer | 8 |
| Basket of dates | 10 |
| Shekel | 1 |

The approved construction requirements total **1,198 points**. Your contribution
percentage is your total construction points divided by 1,198, multiplied by 100.

## Construction leaderboard and reward

Construction contributions are recorded in the neighbourhood
leaderboard under **Rank by → Ziggurat contributions**. Scores sum accepted
donations across all stages. Scores are ordered highest first, with equal scores ordered alphabetically by farmer name and sharing a rank; current neighbours without donations appear with
**0 points**. Refresh or reopen to see new donations. Contributions remain with
the original neighbourhood if the contributor moves. At completion, contributors are ranked by their total construction
points, preserving recognition of the neighbourhood's leading builders.

The number-one contributor receives a decorative miniature ziggurat statue for
their farm and a permanent **Ziggurat Patron** badge when construction is complete.
Awards are saved automatically for every winner, including offline players, once per
completed construction. The statue is kept in **Manage estate → Buildings → Decorations**.
Place it on one free ground tile, then return it to storage from its popup or the
drawer without losing it. Placement and storage cost nothing and keep the farmer free.
The statue fits on one tile and is optional to display. These rewards are cosmetic;
they grant no production or happiness advantage.

If multiple contributors tie for first place, each receives the full statue and
badge reward. The reward is not split and there is no tie-breaker.

## Badges in leaderboards

A **Badges** column is available in both the neighbourhood and global
leaderboards, between Farmer and the selected ranking score. It currently shows
a Beginner Farmer badge for levels 1–9, replaced by the level-10 badge at level
10 and above. The Ziggurat Patron badge appears in both alongside the level badge.
Hover or focus a badge for its Radix tooltip.

Badges recognise achievements without changing the leaderboard's selected score
or rank. The construction ranking remains based on contribution points.

## Benefits for contributors

Every neighbour who makes an accepted construction contribution gains access to
festivals once the ziggurat is complete, regardless of the size of their donation
or their leaderboard rank.

There is no permanent happiness-decay bonus. The ziggurat's gameplay benefits
come through festivals.

## Festivals

Festivals become available only after the ziggurat is complete. Only players
level 10 and above can participate and receive their benefits.

A later addition will let neighbours who missed construction make a donation to
the completed ziggurat to gain festival access. Its cost and flow are not yet
defined. These later donations are separate from construction contributions.

There will be one festival proposal at a time, without voting. Festival choices
will rotate. The first festival will benefit crop growth; additional festival
types and seasonal festivals may be introduced later.

Festival rules will be designed separately after the building flow is settled.
Durations, offerings, crop eligibility and the size of the growth benefit are
not yet defined.

## Editorial: proposals and decisions still to confirm

The following ideas were discussed but are **not approved rules**:

- **Progress presentation:** show contribution points and percentages alongside
  stage resource progress and countdowns. No qualification target is needed.
- **Delivery presentation:** confirm the precise hitching-post position, road route,
  delivery point, loaded/empty donkey artwork and loading/unloading motions in a
  visual preview. Reconcile shared
  active trips after reloads without replaying completed deliveries.
- **Reward artwork review:** approve the generated Patron badge and miniature placement visually. Reward ownership, placement/storage and badge tooltips are implemented.
- **Membership changes:** settle whether relocation is restricted during
  construction and how moving affects festival access and historical recognition.
  The previously discussed relocation lock has not been fully agreed.
- **Post-construction donations:** define their cost and how they unlock festival
  access for non-contributors; do not add them to construction leaderboard points.
- **Festival expansion:** different celebrations could grant increased yields or
  gifts. God-specific themes, seasonal availability and reward rules need a later
  design discussion.
- **Educational content:** short historically verified notes at each stage were
  suggested; wording and presentation are not yet agreed.

The former founding-only eligibility, reserved material shares, qualification
thresholds and permanent 10% slower happiness decay are superseded by open
level-10 participation, construction ranking and festival access for contributors.
The former automatic opening and 48-hour announcement are superseded by three
explicit expressions of interest followed by immediate fundraising.

Implementation must keep economic state server-authoritative: animations must
never delay saved construction or determine whether contributions are consumed.
