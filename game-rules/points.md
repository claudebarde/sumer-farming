# Player points

Points are earned automatically for completed work and accumulate for the lifetime
of the farm. Find your total in Manage estate → Farmer and the farmer’s map popup. The leaderboard ranks by
Points by default; equal scores share a rank and names break display-order ties.

Floating harvest numbers show points: +10 for barley and +20 for sesame.
Keeping a caught fish shows +5 at storage. Other deliveries show no gain number
because moving goods earns no points.

## Approved principles

- Track points from the beginning of the game, including the early farm levels.
- Points accumulate permanently as the player plays. They are a lifetime score, not a spendable currency.
- Reward successfully completed work. Walking, travelling, clicking and opening interfaces earn no points.
- Production earns points per completed action/batch, not per bag, basket, jar or other item produced. This applies to flour, brewer's groats, bread and beer.
- Donkey milling earns slightly more points than farmer milling; the difference must remain small.
- Include point rewards for gaining farm levels.
- Points are the default leaderboard sorting option; other rankings remain available.
- Display points in the Farmer section of Manage estate and the farmer’s map popup, between the happiness and next-meal meters.

## Approved scoring scale

Use whole-number scores: 10 for a barley harvest, 20 for a sesame harvest and
30 for a manual milling batch. Donkey milling earns 35 points per batch, for
both flour and brewer's groats. Its larger output does not multiply this award.

The tables cover current activities. Future crops, industries and festivals need
separate scoring decisions when their rules are agreed.

## Approved action table

### Crops and gathering

| Completed action | Points | Award unit and timing |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Sow barley | 5 | Per field, when sowing completes |
| Harvest ripe barley | 10 | Per field, when cutting completes successfully |
| Harvest withered barley | 10 | Per field; the completed action earns the same score despite its lower yield |
| Sow sesame | 5 | Per field, when sowing completes |
| Harvest sesame | 20 | Per field, when cutting completes successfully; three baskets still mean one award |
| Plant a date palm | 15 | Per completed planting, once per arable coordinate per estate, to avoid replanting for points |
| Tend a date palm | 5 | Per completed tending action within the valid window, at most once per fruit cycle |
| Harvest dates | 20 | Per completed fruit-cycle harvest, not per basket or delivery |
| Catch and keep a fish | 5 | Per successful fishing attempt whose catch is kept; award when the catch is stored |
| Collect reeds | 0 | Low-effort gathering; collecting and destroying resources must not generate points |
| Collect clay | 0 | Low-effort gathering; collecting and destroying resources must not generate points |
| Collect water | 0 | Preparation for brewing; reward the finished brewing batch instead |

Selecting six fields is six field actions, not one selection award. A cancelled,
skipped or failed field earns nothing. A completed field keeps its points if a
later field cannot be completed. Expired barley that clears without harvesting
earns nothing. Automatic crop growth and fruit ripening earn no points.

Harvesting awards happen when the server records the successful harvest; delivery
does not award those points again. Discarding gathered goods or losing harvested
goods later does not create another award or undo an already earned award.
Fishing is deliberately awarded only for kept catches, to avoid catch-and-release
repetition generating points.

### Production

| Completed action | Points | Award unit and timing |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Make flour with the farmer | 30 | Per completed milling batch |
| Make flour with the donkey | 35 | Per completed milling batch, even though the donkey produces more bags |
| Make brewer's groats with the farmer | 30 | Per completed milling batch |
| Make brewer's groats with the donkey | 35 | Per completed milling batch |
| Bake bread | 40 | Per completed baking batch, not per bread basket |
| Brew beer | 40 | Per completed fermentation batch; stirring and supply trips earn no separate award |

Award these points when production finishes, whether the player is watching or
away. Collecting and storing the output adds no extra points. Upgrading a building
changes its production capabilities but does not multiply the points per batch.
Each successfully completed batch earns its own award even when several buildings
are working at once.

### Construction and progression

| Completed action | Points | Award unit and timing |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Complete a road tile | 0 | Building and destroying roads must not generate repeatable points |
| Complete an irrigation canal tile | 0 | Building and destroying canals must not generate repeatable points |
| Build a granary | 50 | First completed granary in the estate |
| Build a mill | 50 | First completed mill in the estate |
| Build a bread oven | 50 | First completed bread oven in the estate |
| Build a brewery | 50 | First completed brewery in the estate |
| Build a small shrine | 50 | First completed small shrine in the estate |
| Build a warehouse | 50 | First completed warehouse in the estate |
| Complete a player-started building upgrade | 50 | Once per building type and attained tier per estate |
| Claim a new farm level | 100 | Once per successfully claimed level, starting with level 2 |
| Automatic granary level/capacity increase | 0 | Included in the farm level-up reward; no separate upgrade work took place |

The first-construction restrictions are approved: demolishing and rebuilding
a building does not reset its construction or upgrade award eligibility. Roads
and canals always earn zero points. Moving or storing a decoration earns nothing. Farm level 1 starts at
zero; its completed activities can immediately earn points. Later level claims
all give the same 100 points in this initial scoring scale.

### Household, requests and cooperation

| Completed action | Points | Award unit and timing |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Give fish to the farmer | 5 | Per successful treat action, respecting its existing cooldown |
| Give bread to the farmer | 5 | Per successful treat action, respecting its existing cooldown |
| Give beer to the farmer | 5 | Per successful treat action, respecting its existing cooldown |
| Give dates to the farmer | 5 | Per successful treat action, respecting its existing cooldown |
| Make a bread offering at the shrine | 10 | Per accepted offering that consumes its goods and starts the blessing |
| Make a beer offering at the shrine | 10 | Per accepted offering that consumes its goods and starts the blessing |
| Fulfil a merchant request | 50 | Per fulfilled request, regardless of quantity or how many goods it contains |
| Help fund a ziggurat stage | 25 | Once per player per stage, awarded when that stage finishes if the player donated to it |
| Complete the ziggurat / earn its Patron rewards | 0 extra | Stage contributions already earn points; badges and decorations keep their own reward rules |

Ziggurat construction contribution points remain separate. They measure donated
materials and determine the construction ranking and Patron rewards. The approved
25 player points do not replace, multiply or alter those contribution points.
Splitting a donation into several transactions gives no additional player points.
A player who contributes to all four stages would earn 100 player points in total.

### Actions without a point award

| Action | Points | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Walk, visit the market or neighbourhood, pan or zoom | 0 | Navigation, not completed productive work |
| Open a popup, click a button, select fields or confirm a job | 0 | Award the eligible outcome later, not the input |
| Pick up, carry, drop, withdraw, transfer or store goods | 0 | Moving the same goods must not generate repeatable points; keeping a fish is the explicit exception above |
| Porter collection, split deliveries or brewery jar restocking | 0 | Support work; no second award for goods already produced |
| Supply barley, flour, groats, water or empty jars to a building | 0 | Included in the eventual production action |
| Buy or sell at NPC stands | 0 | Avoid rewarding buy/sell loops or transaction splitting |
| Create, cancel or fulfil a player-market listing | 0 | Player trading loops can manufacture activity; merchant requests remain separately rewarded |
| Hire a porter or buy a donkey or equipment | 0 | A purchase alone is not completed work; their productive use is rewarded where listed |
| Automatic meals or passive happiness changes | 0 | No completed player work |
| Express interest in ziggurat construction | 0 | Interest alone does not contribute materials |
| Donation delivery animation | 0 | Visual feedback only; the contribution award is counted separately |
| Destroy a building, road, canal or dropped goods | 0 | Avoid demolition/reconstruction reward loops |
| Release a fish, miss a cast or cancel fishing | 0 | No kept catch |
| Dog fetch, idle sweeping, music or cosmetic previews | 0 | Optional or cosmetic activity |
| Sign in, reload, restore a tab or claim an existing reward again | 0 | No new completed outcome |

## Scoring examples

- Sow and harvest one barley field: 5 + 10 = **15 points**. Storing its barley adds zero.
- Sow and harvest one sesame field: 5 + 20 = **25 points**. Its three baskets still earn only one harvest award.
- Complete two manual flour batches and use their flour in one bread batch: 30 + 30 + 40 = **100 points** across three completed actions.
- Make one flour batch with the donkey: **35 points**, not three times the manual award.
- Harvest three sesame fields: **60 points** for the three completed harvests, with no selection or delivery bonus.
- Claim level 10: **100 points**, in addition to points already earned completing the requirements.

## Award timing and existing players

Award points as soon as the eligible action completes successfully. Never wait
for an on-screen animation, a popup or a manual points claim. Production finishes
at its server deadline; harvest work finishes when cutting succeeds. Kept-fish,
accepted treat/offering, level-claim and shared-stage completion events follow
the precise completion conditions in the table.

Reduced-yield harvesting earns the same points as a full-yield harvest: reward
the completed action rather than the number of resources. Failed or cancelled
work earns nothing; retries never award the same completion again.

The sole existing development player's points start at **zero** when tracking
is enabled. Do not backfill historical actions, completed buildings or previous
levels. Seed first-construction and attained-tier eligibility from the existing
estate so rebuilding old buildings cannot claim historical rewards. Newly
completed eligible work after activation earns points normally. Pending work
completed before activation earns no retrospective award.

For future real users, initialize the score at zero when their game begins and
track eligible work from the first level. No later-level unlock gates earning
points. Points become the **default leaderboard sorting option**; retain the
other available ranking options.

## Points icon

The points icon shows a small group of clay accounting tokens: a rounded disc and a short cone,
with a warm terracotta finish and the same soft outlines as the game's icons.
This is a counting/record-keeping motif, distinct from shekels and the clay-tablet
level badge. It represents activity points in the game, not a historical reward currency.

Historical inspiration: [From Accounting to Writing, Denise Schmandt-Besserat,
University of Texas](https://sites.utexas.edu/dsb/tokens/from-accounting-to-writing/)
describes geometric clay tokens used to count goods in the ancient Near East.
The game's icon is a stylized adaptation, not a replica of a specific token.

## Implementation requirements

Keep this section out of player-facing help.

- The server awards points from validated, persisted outcomes, never animation callbacks or client-supplied scores. Saved deadlines are reconciled on farm reads/actions, including after time away; receipts retain their original completion times.
- Award each outcome exactly once, including retries, simultaneous requests, reloads and offline catch-up.
- Record the source and scoring-rule version of awards so duplicate prevention and later balance changes are auditable.
- Each field and each production batch has its own award identity. Delivery splits, output quantity and job retries must not multiply awards.
- Persist one-time construction and tier awards independently of a building's lifetime so demolition does not reset eligibility.
- Keep a central scoring table; update this guide alongside approved values and implementation.
- Preserve existing economy, progression and ziggurat reward rules unless explicitly changed.
